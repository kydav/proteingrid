# Protein Grid: App Store optimization

_Last updated 2026-10-31. Live: v1.0 (June 2026), 1 rating, not in the top results for "protein tracker", "protein counter" or "protein intake"._

## Why it's not ranking
1. **The title doesn't contain the search term.** Apple weighs the title most, and "Protein Grid" never says *tracker* or *counter*. Every app above us has "Protein Tracker" or "Protein Counter" in its title.
2. **One rating.** Ratings count and velocity are the next biggest factor. The leaders have 1.8k–10.8k.
3. **No updates since June.** Recency matters a bit, and "What's New" activity matters more.

## Ratings plan (built in 1.1)
- The native review prompt shows on the **5th goal day**. Apple caps it at 3 times a year.
- Reply to every review in App Store Connect, since replies show publicly.
- Ask friends and family who actually use it to rate it during week 1 of the update. The first 20 ratings move the most.

## Ongoing levers
- **Ship every 2–4 weeks**, each time with a real "What's New".
- **In-App Events** (App Store Connect → In-App Events), e.g. "30-Day Protein Grid Challenge". Events show in search results and Today-tab placements for free.
- **Custom Product Page for GLP-1 users**, with screenshots about protecting muscle on a GLP-1. Use it for any ads and links aimed at r/Semaglutide, r/Zepbound and r/Mounjaro, where protein is a constant topic.
- **Apple Search Ads Basic**, $5–10/day on "protein tracker" for 2 weeks. That tells you the conversion rate and gives Apple's algorithm early install velocity. Stop if cost per install goes over about $1.50.
- **Content:** short videos of the Grid filling in ("I hit my protein goal 300 days this year"). The Grid is the shareable hook.

## Realistic target
This is a niche dominated by Protein Pal (10.8k ratings). With the title change, 50+ ratings and steady updates, page-1 placement for long-tail searches is realistic: "simple protein tracker", "protein counter no account", "protein grid", "glp1 protein". At a 2–4% trial-to-paid rate, ~2,000 installs a month is roughly 40–80 Pro buyers a month, about **$300–700/mo** after Apple's 15% (small business program). That's a small but real side income.
